What is Hermes?

Hermes is the local AI layer that runs on your machine and works with your Coinbase setup.

It is not a hosted black box. It runs locally, keeps your credentials on your system, and uses AI-assisted analysis to help you review market conditions.

Think of it as a local research and automation layer for your Coinbase account — not a set-and-forget bot that vanishes into someone else’s cloud.

Why use Hermes for trading?

  • Local dashboard and logs you can inspect.
  • Credential sync stays inside your app profile.
  • AI-assisted reasoning for market review and follow-up checks.
  • Scheduled checks through cron-style jobs.
  • Direct Coinbase Advanced Trade integration.
Important: KnightTrader Coinbase is a trading assistant, not financial advice. Always review trades before relying on them. Start small, verify your API permissions, and keep your API keys restricted.
