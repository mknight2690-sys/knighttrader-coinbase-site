FAQ

Is this financial advice?

No. KnightTrader is a tool you control. Trade at your own risk after verifying all settings.

How is my subscription verified?

The app periodically checks your subscription status with our backend using an encrypted token. This happens 24/7 to ensure only subscribers can use the software.

Where do my API keys go?

Your Coinbase API keys are stored encrypted on your machine and synced to your local Hermes setup.

Can I use this without a subscription?

No. A valid $47/month subscription is required to download and use KnightTrader Coinbase.

What if I cancel my subscription?

If your subscription lapses, you'll lose access to downloads and updates. Your local credentials remain saved.

Important: This is a trading assistant, not financial advice. You should review your setup, API permissions, and risk. Start small, verify your Coinbase API permissions, and keep keys restricted.
