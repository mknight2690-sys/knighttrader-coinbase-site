What is Hermes?

Hermes is the local AI assistant on your machine. In this setup, it becomes your trading assistant on Coinbase.

If you are new to AI agents, think of it like a local assistant that you can review and steer. If you have used agents or automation before, think of it as a configurable local runtime with persistent memory and scheduled execution.

It is not a hosted bot you cannot inspect. It runs on your computer, keeps your credentials local, and executes scheduled trading checks through the Hermes dashboard.

Persistent learning

Hermes is built to remember. It writes durable memory notes for important trades and outcomes, so later runs can learn from earlier ones instead of starting blind.

That means the assistant can refine strategy over time and keep a trace of what it did, rather than treating each tick as a fresh unknown.

Strategy improvement

  • It remembers past trades and context.

FAQ

I am new to crypto. Is this for me?

Yes. The app includes a How-To tab that explains each step. You can follow along even if you have not used a trading bot or AI assistant before.

I have used trading bots before. Is this different?

It is local-first and inspectable. You can review logs, prompts, and memory files instead of treating it as an opaque hosted service.

Do I need a VPN?

No. KnightTrader Coinbase runs locally and talks to Coinbase directly.

Do I have to place trades myself?

No. After setup, the assistant can trade on your behalf. You can inspect logs and the dashboard anytime.

Where do my API keys go?

They stay on your machine. The app stores credentials locally and syncs them into your local Hermes setup.

What if I need help?

Open the How-To tab inside the app. It walks through Hermes setup, API keys, first-time configuration, and keeping your computer awake.

Is this a subscription or hosted service?

No. It is a local app with one-time setup. There is no ongoing account with us beyond the Coinbase API you control.

What should I do if the download button breaks?

Use the Open release page link below the main download button and grab the installer for your platform manually from the latest release.
