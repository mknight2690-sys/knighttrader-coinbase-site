What is Hermes?

Hermes is the local AI agent that works on your machine. In this setup, it becomes your trading assistant on Coinbase.

It is not a hosted bot you cannot inspect. It runs on your computer, keeps your credentials local, and executes scheduled trading checks through the Hermes dashboard.

Think of it as onboarding a trading employee: you install it, configure it once, and it keeps working.

What you are getting

  • A local trading agent that runs every 5 minutes.
  • Continuous operation while your PC is awake and online.
  • No VPN, no ongoing manual decisions, no extra headaches.
  • A simple How-To tab inside the app.
  • A local dashboard and logs you can watch.

Persistent learning

Hermes is built to remember. It writes durable memory notes for important trades and outcomes, so later runs can learn from earlier ones instead of starting blind.

That means the agent can refine strategy over time and keep a trace of what it did, rather than treating each tick as a fresh unknown.

Strategy improvement

  • It remembers past trades and context.
  • You can edit prompts and strategy notes.
  • It can converge on better behavior after fewer turns.
  • Skills and lessons are kept locally.
  • You are effectively training your own agent.
